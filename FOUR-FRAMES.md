## 0. Qualification

This is a personal set of interpretive frames, with known weaknesses and gaps. I use them simply to try to make sense of what's what. They are scaffolding for understanding, not analysis of record, not prediction and not advice. Each frame is limited and biased; the frames are chosen partly so that their biases collide. I expect to revise or discard elements as evidence accumulates, and the entry carries a standing bias register (Section 8) for that purpose.

**Author's note.** This entry exists because I am struggling to keep up. Twenty-five years of practice built categories that the post-Covid world has been outrunning, and I do not believe the struggle is mine alone. There are no right answers in this file. The struggle to frame is itself the work, and it should stay difficult: if these frames ever start to feel comfortable, treat the comfort as a finding and suspect capture.

**Premise, stated so it can be attacked.** Humans are narrative cognizers, evolved for a complicated and uncertain world by compressing it into story, and analysts run on narratives whether or not they admit them. This method runs them explicitly, written and scored. Narrative-based analysis will not suit every practitioner, and it does not need to: the standard instruments run with discipline reach the same upstream questions. The entry's purpose is deeper analysis, by whichever door the analyst takes.

Operating rule: a claim that survives every frame in the portfolio is worth acting on. A claim that exists in only one frame is probably an artifact of that frame.

The surviving set is not empty. As of this writing, three claims hold in all four frames: middle-power hedging becomes the default posture, chokepoint leverage rises in value and trust becomes the scarce asset. Cross-frame survivors of this kind are what the method promotes to register-grade.

**Position as of 2026-08-23, stated so it cannot be mistaken.** On one scored exhibit, the evidence leans away from restoration (Greek down) and toward rupture with interregnum (Norse and Mandate-interregnum up), with the Kali reading standing as the check on both. That lean is a reading under revision, not a verdict. The three survivors above are the only claims this entry promotes to action.

## 1. Method

- Each frame is a hypothesis about system dynamics. Frames generate questions and indicators, never conclusions.
- The four are deliberately drawn from different civilizational vantages: two Western (Greek, Norse), one Indic (Kali Yuga), one Sinic (Mandate of Heaven). Each checks the others. The two non-Western frames exist specifically to stress-test the Western pair, including the v0.1 core thesis (US as Loki).
- Mappings and indicators are pre-registered here, dated, then scored against events. Retrofitting myth to events after the fact is prohibited; myth is rich enough to fit anything.
- Weight shifts between frames as discriminating evidence arrives (Section 7). No frame wins permanently.
- Known failure mode: with four frames everything is explainable. The discipline is the discriminator set, not the frames.
- The portfolio is personal by design and the frames are replaceable; the discipline is not. Users of this method should draft frames from traditions they hold deeply rather than adopting these four. A frame borrowed thin produces caricature; a frame you were raised in can correct you. Traditions include constructed ones: a game world inhabited over years is also held deeply.
- Admission test for a new frame, five parts: (1) a real theory of order dynamics (how power is gained, held, lost and renewed), not just imagery; (2) drawn from a tradition the author knows well enough to be corrected in; (3) disagrees with at least one incumbent frame on a question evidence can settle, since a frame that always agrees with your instincts is a mirror; (4) generates observable indicators; (5) its known misuses are documented beside it.
- Retirement rule: a frame that has not moved portfolio weight across four consecutive scoring cycles, or whose indicators cannot discriminate it from an incumbent frame, is demoted to archive.

## 2. Frame I: Greek pantheon (homeostatic order)

**System claim.** The order survives; power redistributes within it. The founding war (Titanomachy) is settled, domains are partitioned (sky, sea, underworld) and the system engine is hubris followed by Nemesis: overreach draws punishment and equilibrium restores. This is balance-of-power realism in mythic dress. Thucydides belongs to this cultural frame, including the Melian aphorism about the strong and the weak.

**Key figures and mechanisms.**

- Zeus/Metis/Athena: the hegemon swallows cunning intelligence (Metis) to prevent a superior successor, and Athena bursts out of his skull fully armed. Exact figure for AI containment: intelligence swallowed is not contained, only incubated, and it emerges armored from inside the container.
- Poseidon and Hades: domain partition as strategy (maritime power; the underworld of cyber and shadow finance).
- Prometheus: unauthorized technology transfer and its punishment (open diffusion, proliferation).
- Hermes: the licensed connector role (entrepôt and mediator states).
- Nemesis: the load-bearing figure. The Greek frame holds only if overreach still gets punished by the system.
- Themis: divine law and right order, the rules half of the pair Nemesis enforces. The embedded indicator: whether rules are invoked as actual constraint or only as decoration. The EU reads most naturally as the Themis-aligned actor.
- Melos: the middle power in the path of the strong. The Melian Dialogue is the frame's warning label for middle powers, and middle-power strategy in Greek terms is the art of not being Melos. Carney's Davos program reads as exactly that art.
- Cassandra: accurate warning, disbelieved by construction. The figure of early-warning failure; track warnings issued against warnings acted on.
- The Titans in Tartarus: defeated powers are imprisoned, not destroyed, and the frame quietly assumes the prison holds. Stored revanchism is the Greek frame's blind spot; Russia reads naturally here.
- The Delian slide: the league that became an empire once the common treasury moved to Athens. The alliance-to-extraction gradient; burden-sharing disputes are its live marker.
- Ostracism: the institutionalized removal of the over-mighty before they break the system. Modern analogues (term limits, antitrust, targeted sanctions) and their decay are a Nemesis-health indicator.

**Reading of today.** Hegemonic transition inside a surviving order; institutions bend, absorb and persist; succession anxiety drives the hegemon's technology controls (the Metis move).

**Questions the frame forces.** Name the last three overreaches the system actually punished, with dates. Is Themis still seated: are rules being invoked as constraint or as decoration? Who is the Metis being swallowed this cycle, and what will burst out armored? Is Tartarus holding? Where on the Delian slide is each alliance, and who holds the treasury?

**If this frame is right.** The order bends and absorbs. Tariff walls get litigated, arbitraged and partially rolled back; overreach draws coalition and market punishment; institutions reform late, but reform. By 2030 the system looks battered and familiar. GRC posture: incumbent-architecture compliance keeps paying; resilience-test institutions rather than replacing them; enforcement and snap-back assumptions remain valid.

**Indicators (Greek-world evidence).** Institutions absorb shocks and continue functioning; overreach draws effective systemic punishment; parallel systems stay subscale; crisis response still runs through incumbent architecture; rules still invoked as constraint (Themis seated); contained powers stay contained (Tartarus holds).

**Operational translation (GRC).** Concentration and overreach risk; resilience testing of incumbent institutions; assume enforcement and snap-back mechanisms continue to operate.

**What it sees that the others miss.** An enforcement mechanism. Only the Greek frame has a working Nemesis; the other three explain decline but not discipline.

**Frame bias.** Status-quo bias; analytically punishes disruptors; assumes a functioning Nemesis that may no longer exist.

## 3. Frame II: Norse pantheon (entropic, actor-driven rupture)

**System claim.** The order is mortal and knows it. Strategy under foreknown rupture: not prevention but preparation. Critical nuance: Ragnarök is not termination. Völuspá ends with a green world, surviving gods and Baldr's return. The strategic question is therefore not who wins the current game but who is accumulating post-rupture assets.

**Role registry and stage markers (roles, not states; states and institutions migrate between roles).**

- Odin (order-architect): formerly the US. Possibly vacant. An order with no Odin is itself a diagnosis.
- Loki (endogenous chaos agent): the v0.1 core thesis, held as role migration, not identity. Loki is inside Asgard by blood-oath; he procured the order's defining instruments, then turned against it. Six-stage arc for placement: (1) useful trickster, (2) malicious but reparable, (3) irreversible norm-kill by guided hands and withheld assent (Baldr), (4) systematic public delegitimization of peers (Lokasenna, corrosive because the insults are largely true), (5) bound and thrashing, (6) rupture combatant.
- Rival casting to stress-test: Cronus, the founder who devours his own institutional children to deny them to a successor. Discriminator: Cronus predicts targeted destruction of successor-empowering institutions; Loki predicts generalized entropy and intrigue.
- Tyr and Gleipnir (credible binding): the EU. A silken ribbon forged from intangibles that holds where chains failed: regulatory soft power. Two embedded warnings: the binder pays a hand, and the binding holds only until the wolf tests it at full strength.
- Fenrir (the bound rising power): China from the order's vantage. Raised inside the order, feared, bound with intangible chokepoints, and the binding manufactures the prophesied enmity. Containment as self-fulfillment. From Fenrir's own vantage the story is betrayal of a creature raised as a pet: structurally the century-of-humiliation narrative.
- Jörmungandr (globalization): the serpent holding the world together by biting its own tail. Decoupling is the serpent releasing its tail: an end-signal.
- Baldr and the mistletoe: the norm all swore to protect, killed through the one unbound exception. Candidate: "interdependence prevents great-power war." Open questions: has the Baldr event occurred, what was the mistletoe, who is Thökk (the actor whose withheld assent blocks restoration).
- Heimdallr (the watcher), Surtr (the actor whose only lever is burning), Hel (the sanctioned shadow half-world), the dwarves (the unworshipped forges: deep supply chains), the Norns (the fate layer above all agency: demography, climate, technology diffusion).
- Fimbulwinter (the stage before the end): three winters with no summer between, and in Gylfaginning and Völuspá the social symptom precedes the climatic one: brothers fight and kill each other, kinship bonds break, an axe-age and sword-age before the world falls. The frame's most underused instrument. Internal fracture precedes external rupture, which makes domestic polarization and intra-alliance fratricide leading indicators on the Ragnarök track, not separate phenomena.
- Mímir: the beheaded counselor whose head Odin preserves and consults. Institutional expertise kept artificially alive, advisory and disembodied: consulted, no longer governing. A figure for the fate of expert institutions inside a Loki-run order.

**Questions the frame forces.** Are we in Fimbulwinter: is kin-against-kin fracture (domestic polarization, intra-Western conflict) accelerating ahead of external rupture? Where is the US on the Loki arc, and what dated evidence would move it one stage? Has Baldr died: which norm, was the kill irreversible and who is Thökk? What belongs in the einherjar portfolio: which alliances, standards and reserves survive a rupture? Has anyone yet tested Gleipnir at full strength?

**If this frame is right.** The order's authors finish dismantling it. Parallel payment, security and standards rails reach scale; a rupture event, financial, military or constitutional, partitions the system; the pre-positioned coalition rebuilds a smaller order on the far side. By 2030 the question is not whether the old system returns but who boarded the new one early. GRC posture: dependency maps with executable exits; contracts stress-tested for treaty lapse; survivor-set review of assets, alliances and standards.

**Indicators (Norse-world evidence).** Decay concentrated in the order's core and driven by identifiable insiders; pre-positioning for post-order life at scale (alternative payment rails, reserve diversification, treaty-exit cascades, hedging as default alliance posture); weaponization of the order's own instruments by its author; kin-against-kin leading indicators (domestic polarization, intra-alliance conflict) per the Fimbulwinter stage.

**Operational translation (GRC).** Map dependencies on order-contingent infrastructure (dollar clearing, treaty coverage, standards bodies); rupture scenario planning; review which assets and relationships belong to the survivor set.

**What it sees that the others miss.** Insider agency. Only the Norse frame prices the possibility that the system's own architect takes it down, and only it makes pre-positioning rational rather than disloyal.

**Frame bias.** Fatalism (prophecy makes rupture-evidence salient and continuity-evidence invisible); actor-centrism; Western agonism (casts rivals as monsters). Both non-Western frames below attack this frame directly.

## 4. Frame III: Kali Yuga (systemic decay, civilizational cycle)

**Mechanics.** Indic cosmology runs time through four ages (yugas) of declining dharma, pictured as a bull losing legs: four in the age of truth, one in the last age, Kali Yuga, the age of discord (the quarters in Manusmriti 1.81-86; the bull in Bhagavata Purana 1.16-17). Kali Yuga ends in dissolution and renewal through an exogenous agent (Kalki), not through a war among peers. The Puranic symptom lists are strikingly usable as institutional-decay indicators. Paraphrasing Bhagavata Purana 12.2 and Vishnu Purana Book VI: rulers become predators and revenue-extractors rather than protectors; wealth alone confers rank and virtue; justice tracks power; success in commerce runs on deceit; learning is commodified and credentials replace substance; family and institutional bonds reduce to convenience; speech degrades and truthfulness becomes rare.

**What the frame encodes.** Decay is a property of the age, not of an actor. There is no Loki here: no protagonist, no villain, no monster. Every actor degrades together, and rivalrous framing is itself a symptom. Under this frame the US is not a chaos agent; US conduct is a symptom of the age, as is its rivals' conduct, as is the commentary about both.

**Working parts, beyond the symptom list.**

- The four feet, as a dashboard. In the Bhagavata account the bull Dharma stands on austerity, purity, compassion and truthfulness, and the age of discord breaks three, leaving truth standing last and under attack. Read as institutional dimensions: austerity is discipline (fiscal and personal restraint), purity is integrity (anti-corruption), compassion is solidarity (social trust and mutual obligation) and truthfulness is the epistemic commons. The construction even yields a testable hypothesis about breaking order, discipline eroding first and truth last (the texts themselves do not fix the sequence; the dashboard does): if the epistemic commons collapses while fiscal discipline holds, the hypothesis fails.
- Yuga-sandhi: the chronology includes junction periods between ages. The frame carries its own concept of interregnum, not a fight over succession but a twilight in which two ages overlap. It resonates with, and independently checks, the Mandate frame's interregnum reading.
- Kalki, held strictly as a placeholder: renewal arrives from outside every incumbent's strategy. The frame refuses to specify what a Kalki-class reset looks like, which is the point: no actor gets to flatter its own project as the renewal.

**Two vantage checks this frame performs.**

1. It dissolves the v0.1 core thesis. If Kali Yuga is the better fit, "US as Loki" is a category error: attributing to agency what belongs to entropy.
2. It denies the baseline. Classical chronology places the whole modern era deep inside Kali Yuga. The frame never accepted 1945-2020 as normalcy; it reads the liberal-order decades as a local plateau inside a long decline. This checks Western declinism, which silently treats the 1990s as the natural state of the world.

**Strategy implication.** Not victory and not restoration: preservation. In a degenerating age the rational strategy is building arks: institutions in miniature that carry order through the dark (canons, standards, professional ethics, auditable systems, communities of practice). Renewal is expected but distant and exogenous; no actor's strategy produces it. Note for GRCnext: this frame assigns the assurance profession a specific role. Audit, certification and verification are dharma-preservation vehicles: mechanisms for keeping truthfulness expensive to abandon in an information order where it is degrading.

**Questions the frame forces.** Is decay symmetric: run the same trust, corruption and rule-of-law series across the US, EU, China and the middle powers, and does any actor decouple from the decline? Which of the four feet is breaking fastest this year, and which institutions function as arks for that specific dimension? What was commodified this year that was not last year? And the reflexive one: is this analysis, and the commentary economy it belongs to, accelerating the decay it measures?

**If this frame is right.** No rupture event ever arrives; the drama of rupture is itself a symptom. Everything degrades together, rules and discourse, incumbents and challengers alike, and the world of 2030 is not partitioned but coarsened. The scarce asset is verified truth, and the institutions that survive best are small, disciplined and auditable. GRC posture: integrity becomes the product; assurance and verification become core infrastructure rather than overhead; build for century-scale durability and accept that no strategy of victory exists.

**Indicators (Kali-world evidence).** Institutional-trust decline that is symmetric across rivals (US, EU, China, others) rather than concentrated in the order's core; erosion of the epistemic commons (truth-decay metrics, synthetic media prevalence); commodification creep into formerly non-market domains; elite predation and corruption indices rising across systems simultaneously; the four-feet dashboard tracked as separate series (fiscal discipline, corruption, social trust, epistemic integrity) with the predicted breaking order as the test.

**Operational translation (GRC).** Integrity and trust become the scarce assets; assurance and auditability are preservation infrastructure, not overhead; design institutions for century-scale durability rather than cycle-scale advantage.

**What it sees that the others miss.** The moral substrate, and the observer. Only the Kali frame treats symmetric decay as primary rather than as noise, and only it audits the analyst as part of the phenomenon.

**Frame bias and misuse.** Quietism (if the age is fallen, why act); timescale mismatch (a civilizational clock answers none of this quarter's questions); and political capture. The Kali Yuga vocabulary has been appropriated by Traditionalist and far-right currents (Evola and successors) as a license for anti-modern politics. Using the frame requires explicit distance from that reading: here it is a decay-symptom checklist and a preservation ethic, nothing more.

## 5. Frame IV: Mandate of Heaven (legitimacy transfer, dynastic cycle)

**Mechanics.** In the Sinic frame, Heaven (tian) grants the mandate to rule (tianming) conditionally on performance: order, prosperity, protection, competent response to disasters. Misrule and calamity signal withdrawal; rebellion against a failed ruler is legitimate (Mencius 1B.8: deposing a ruler who has forfeited the mandate is not regicide, merely the removal of a "mere fellow"). The origin is the Zhou justification for overthrowing the Shang (Book of Documents). The cycle: rise through vigor and virtue, consolidation, peak, decadence, disasters and defection, fall, new claimant. Between dynasties: interregnum. The opening line of Romance of the Three Kingdoms states the whole frame: the empire, long divided, must unite; long united, must divide.

**What the frame encodes.** Legitimacy is the unit of account, and it is performance-based and revocable. A transfer requires two things at once: incumbent failure and claimant performance. Incumbent failure alone yields not transfer but interregnum (Warring States conditions: protracted multipolarity, self-strengthening, shifting coalitions).

**Working parts, beyond the cycle.**

- The censorate: the institutionalized right of remonstrance, officials whose function is to tell the throne it is wrong. In the frame's logic, late dynasties muzzle it before they fall. Functional analogues: inspectors general, audit institutions, courts, a free press. The embedded indicator: can anyone inside each principal still say no, on the record, and survive it professionally?
- The tribute system: vassals trade ritual deference for trade access and protection, and the bargain fails when extraction exceeds protection. This is Carney's "hegemons cannot continually monetize their relationships" stated as dynastic mechanics. The embedded indicator: the tribute-to-protection ratio across each alliance system.
- Rebel or bandit: legitimacy is retrospective. The winning claimant becomes the Son of Heaven and the loser a bandit (the proverb runs: winners become kings, losers become bandits). During interregnum, contenders are formally indistinguishable, which is why third-party alignment, not principal rhetoric, is the only readable evidence.
- The warlords: the interregnum's protagonists, self-strengthening and coalition-shifting. Today's middle powers, with variable geometry as the modern form of Warring States diplomacy.

**Vantage value: this is the rival's own operating frame.** Chinese strategic culture runs on dynastic-cycle memory and tianxia (all-under-heaven) concepts, revived in contemporary Chinese IR theory (Zhao Tingyang). Whether the Party leadership itself reasons in tianming terms is contested among specialists; the claim here is deliberately weaker: the cycle is the civilizational memory the system's narratives draw on. Xi's stock phrase "great changes unseen in a century" is a mandate-shift claim. Wang Huning's America Against America (1991) reads as a mandate-auditor's field notes on incumbent decay, written by the man who became the system's chief ideologue. Modeling the rival's self-model has predictive value: expect mandate-audition behavior (infrastructure provision, order-brokering, mediation, disaster diplomacy) and read assertiveness as a claim that the transfer window is open.

**The frame cuts both ways.** The late-dynasty symptom checklist (fiscal exhaustion, elite factionalism, court intrigue around succession, suppression of remonstrance, over-centralization on one figure, failure before disasters) must be applied to the claimant with the same rigor as to the incumbent. On several of these the claimant scores as poorly as the incumbent, which is precisely what pushes the reading from transfer toward interregnum.

**Modern translation.** Pax Americana as a dynasty holding a de facto tianxia. Mandate evidence is read in third parties, not in the principals: allied narrative defection (the hearts of the people shifting), Global South alignment votes, currency invoicing, infrastructure uptake. Heaven's audits are the disasters: pandemics, climate events, financial crises. The polity that handles them retains or earns mandate; both principals arguably failed the last audit.

**Questions the frame forces.** What did the last disaster-audit actually show about incumbent versus claimant performance, and what will the next one (climate, financial, epidemic) show? Are third parties realigning toward a claimant or hedging against everyone? Is the censorate function alive in each principal? What is the tribute-to-protection ratio doing in each alliance system?

**If this frame is right (interregnum branch).** No transfer occurs for a generation. Both principals carry late-dynasty symptoms, neither performs the mandate, and the operating system of world politics becomes the interregnum itself: variable-geometry coalitions, issue-by-issue legitimacy, middle powers as protagonists. By 2030 compliance means interoperating across rival regulatory blocs rather than under one hegemonic standard. GRC posture: legitimacy indicators for every jurisdiction of operation; multi-bloc interoperability as a design requirement; disaster response watched as the audit that moves weights.

**Indicators (Mandate-world evidence).** Transfer reading: incumbent late-dynasty symptoms plus claimant performance plus third-party realignment toward the claimant. Interregnum reading: incumbent symptoms plus claimant stall plus generalized hedging with no realignment. Watch disaster-response performance gaps, defection narratives from core allies, censorate health in each principal and the tribute-to-protection ratio.

**Operational translation (GRC).** Legitimacy as a measurable variable: performance-based license to operate; track third-party realignment, not principal rhetoric; treat disaster response as the audit that moves regime weightings.

**What it sees that the others miss.** Legitimacy as a transferable asset, and third parties as the instrument that measures it.

**Frame bias and misuse.** It imports the incumbent Chinese state's legitimating ideology (performance legitimacy as sufficient, consent and rights erased); it carries victor's-history bias (each dynasty wrote its predecessor's history to prove the transfer was foretold); it personifies eras. Used here strictly as an analytic cycle model, not an endorsement of its normative content.

## 6. Exhibit: Carney's Rupture, read four ways

**The datum.** Mark Carney, Prime Minister of Canada, World Economic Forum, Davos, Monday 19 January 2026 (full transcript published by CBS News, 21 January 2026). Verified key language:

> "Today, I will talk about a rupture in the world order, the end of a pleasant fiction and the beginning of a harsh reality..."

> "This bargain no longer works. Let me be direct. We are in the midst of a rupture, not a transition."

> "We know the old order is not coming back. We shouldn't mourn it. Nostalgia is not a strategy."

> "Hegemons cannot continually monetize their relationships."

Structure of the speech: Havel's greengrocer and "living within a lie"; the rules-based order as a useful fiction all parties performed; the hegemon now using "economic integration as weapons, tariffs as leverage, financial infrastructure as coercion"; a middle-power program of self-strengthening (doubling defense spending, joining EU defense procurement, twelve trade and security agreements in six months, a proposed CPTPP-EU bridge of 1.5 billion people, critical-minerals buyers' clubs, AI cooperation so middle powers are not "forced to choose between hegemons and hyper-scalers") and "variable geometry": different coalitions for different issues. He also concluded new strategic partnerships with China and Qatar in the same week, and he cited Thucydides' Melian aphorism by name in order to reject its inevitability.

**Reading 1: Greek.** Carney invokes the frame's own author (Thucydides) and then rejects the frame's central promise: he denies that Nemesis will restore equilibrium ("the old order is not coming back"). A sitting G7 head of government publicly abandoning the restoration hypothesis is a datum against an operative Nemesis function. Greek-frame weight: down.

**Reading 2: Norse.** "A rupture, not a transition" is the Ragnarök claim in five words, and "from the fracture, we can build something bigger" is Ragnarök-as-renewal. His program is einherjar-gathering: assembling the coalition that survives the rupture. His description of the hegemon weaponizing its own instruments corroborates the v0.1 Loki-migration thesis from an unusually qualified witness. Casting note: a former central banker, the watcher class of the old order, sounding the horn is Heimdallr behavior. Norse-frame weight: up.

**Reading 3: Kali Yuga.** The speech's spine is Havel: ritual performance of a known fiction, "living within a lie." That is truth-decay symptomology, and the prescription (live the truth, build what you claim to believe in, make honesty affordable by reducing vulnerability) is a preservation ethic. The frame adds a warning the other frames miss: naming the fiction accelerates its dissolution. The speech is simultaneously diagnosis and symptom-accelerant. Kali-frame weight: partial confirmation.

**Reading 4: Mandate of Heaven.** A principal ally publicly declaring the dynasty's bargain void ("this bargain no longer works") is textbook mandate-withdrawal evidence: the hearts of the people shifting, read in third parties rather than principals. But note what Carney does not do: he confers mandate on no claimant. Hedged partnership with China is insurance, not allegiance. Combined with his self-strengthening program and variable-geometry coalitions, this is Warring States interregnum statecraft, not dynastic transfer. Mandate-frame weight: up for the interregnum sub-reading, flat for the transfer sub-reading.

**Method demonstration.** One datum produces four different indicator updates, two of them in tension (actor-driven rupture vs symptomatic decay). That divergence is the model working as intended. New indicator class registered: declarations by watcher-class figures (central bankers, standard-setters, auditors of the old order) as leading indicators of elite pre-positioning.

**Discriminating indicators (pre-registered 2026-08-23).**

1. Institutions absorb the current shocks and overreach draws effective punishment: weight to Greek.
2. Decay concentrated in the order's core, driven by insiders, with post-order pre-positioning at scale: weight to Norse.
3. Decay symmetric across all major actors including challengers: weight to Kali Yuga.
4. Incumbent decay plus claimant performance plus third-party realignment: weight to Mandate (transfer). Incumbent decay plus claimant stall plus generalized hedging: weight to Mandate (interregnum).
5. Loki vs Cronus (within Norse): generalized entropy vs targeted destruction of successor-empowering institutions.
6. Current load of the Carney datum: Norse and Mandate-interregnum up; Greek down; Kali partial.

**Candidate observable series (indicator to instrument).** Named so that scoring stays tied to data rather than mood. Illustrative, not exhaustive; link specific series before first scoring.

## 9. Placement among standard tools (PESTLE, STEEPLE)

**Ecosystem position.** This entry lives in the grc repository, the method foundation of the rolldabones ecosystem (Layer 1: Doctrine and method). Functionally it behaves like a field study: it tests a sense-making discipline against the live geopolitical system and feeds corrections back into method. It is used alongside the standard macro-environment scanning frameworks, not instead of them. The three account doctrines apply as stated in [DOCTRINE.md](https://github.com/rolldabones/rolldabones/blob/main/DOCTRINE.md); this entry adds instruments, not doctrine.

**What the taxonomies do.** PESTLE partitions the external macro-environment into six factor categories (Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental); STEEPLE extends the set with an Ethical lens addressing corporate values and reputation. Their strengths are completeness, communicability and audit-friendliness: nothing goes unscanned, and the output is legible to any board.

**What taxonomies cannot do.** A taxonomy is static. It carries no view of system dynamics: whether the environment being scanned is stable, decaying or changing hands, how factors interact, or which trajectory the signals are loading into. PESTLE files Carney's Davos speech under Political and stops there. In fairness, skilled practitioners extend PESTLE with trend and interaction analysis; the framework supplies the categories and the practitioner supplies the dynamics. This method makes that supplied layer explicit, written and scoreable.

**Division of labor.**

1. Scan with the taxonomy (PESTLE or STEEPLE): coverage, categorization, completeness.
2. Load the signals into the frames of the portfolio: each frame converts the same signals into a trajectory hypothesis.
3. Score against the pre-registered discriminators (Section 7): weights shift between frames.
4. Translate the weighted reading into GRC work product: risk registers, scenario plans, dependency maps and legitimacy indicators (the operational translations in Sections 2 through 5).

Coverage without dynamics is inventory. Dynamics without coverage is anecdote. The pairing is the method.

**Caution.** PESTLE and STEEPLE are consensus tools; the frames are interpretive and personal. The Section 0 qualification and the Section 8 bias register travel with this method wherever it is used, including any client-facing or board-facing derivative. Audience rule: the frames are upstream instruments for the analyst. Boards and clients see the outputs (registers, scenarios, indicators), never the mythology; if the pantheon reaches a board deck, the method has been misused.
